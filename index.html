Bed bugs in apartments

Bed bugs arrive in luggage, used furniture and even shopping bags, then hide in mattress seams, bed frames and skirting boards. They feed at night and leave itchy welts and rust-coloured spots on sheets. Effective treatment means inspecting the bed and bedroom thoroughly, treating harbourage with residual products, and a follow-up visit timed to the egg-hatch cycle. One visit rarely finishes the job.

Cockroaches in kitchens and garbage rooms

German cockroaches love warm kitchens — behind fridges, under sinks, inside cupboard hinges. In towers they spread between units through plumbing voids. Gel baiting placed deep in harbourage works better than broadcast spraying, and building management should treat garbage rooms and corridors at the same time, or the problem just moves around. https://bitepestcontrol.com

Restaurants and food outlets

Food businesses on Reem Island face municipality inspections and need documented pest control. A proper programme includes scheduled visits, bait stations, fly control and service reports kept on file. Kitchens need treatments timed outside service hours, with products suited to food-preparation areas.

Ants, flies and general apartment care

Sugar ants enter through balcony doors and window frames; drain flies breed in bathroom drains. Small, targeted fixes — sealing, baiting, drain treatment — solve most apartment issues without heavy spraying.
